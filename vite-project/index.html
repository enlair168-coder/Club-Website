About Us

Character first. Service always.

The Integrity and Dignity Club is a student leadership organization built on one conviction: strong communities are built by people who choose honesty, treat others with respect, and take responsibility for the change they want to see.

Through workshops, service projects, and open dialogue, we help students practice these values in their studies, friendships, and communities.

Our Vision

A campus and community where integrity and dignity guide every decision.

Our Mission

To develop principled student leaders through training, service, and honest conversation.
